Support

Need help with Blessie? Contact support for account access, prayer groups, privacy requests, safety reports, or app issues.

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Email Blessie support at support@blessie.ca. Please include the email address connected to your Blessie account when possible.

Email support

What We Can Help With

  • Account access, sign-in issues, and profile questions.
  • Prayer group invitations, group membership, and shared prayer questions.
  • Public prayer requests, reports, blocks, and safety concerns.
  • Privacy questions, data requests, and account deletion requests.
  • App bugs, crashes, notifications, location permissions, or display issues.

Send Useful Details

To help us respond faster, include:

  • Your Blessie username or display name.
  • The device and app platform you are using, such as iPhone, Android, or web.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected to happen.
  • Screenshots or invite codes if they are relevant to the issue.

Account and Privacy

For account deletion, use the instructions on the account deletion page. For information about data collection and your choices, review the privacy policy.

Safety Reports

You can report prayer posts and group posts in the app. For urgent safety concerns, email support@blessie.ca and include enough detail for us to identify the post, group, or account involved.
