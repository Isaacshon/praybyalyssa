Child Safety Standards

Blessie is committed to protecting children and maintaining standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Child safety contact: thswndrnr80@gmail.com

Zero-Tolerance Standard

Blessie strictly prohibits any content, behavior, or account activity that creates, uploads, shares, requests, promotes, solicits, normalizes, or facilitates CSAE or CSAM. This includes child grooming, sexualized content involving minors, exploitation, trafficking, sextortion, or attempts to move a child into unsafe communication.

User Reporting

Blessie allows users to report prayer posts and group posts in the app. Users can also block an author so future posts from that author are hidden from their account.

For urgent child safety concerns, users can also email thswndrnr80@gmail.com. Reports should include the post, group, username, date, and any context that helps us identify the concern.

Review and Enforcement

When Blessie receives a child safety report, we review it and may take actions including:

  • Removing or restricting reported content.
  • Suspending or deleting accounts involved in CSAE or CSAM activity.
  • Preserving information needed for safety review, legal compliance, or law enforcement requests.
  • Reporting suspected CSAM or child exploitation to appropriate regional or national authorities when required by applicable law.

Compliance

Blessie complies with applicable child safety laws and Google Play child safety requirements. When we become aware of suspected CSAE or CSAM, we take appropriate action and report to relevant authorities where required or appropriate, including regional or national reporting bodies and law enforcement.

Contact for Google Play

The designated point of contact for Google Play child safety notifications and CSAE compliance questions is thswndrnr80@gmail.com.
