Delete Your Blessie Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Blessie account and associated data, even if you no longer have access to the app.

Last updated: May 28, 2026

To request account deletion, email support@blessie.ca from the email address connected to your Blessie account and include the subject line "Delete my Blessie account".

Email deletion request

In-App Deletion

  1. Open Blessie.
  2. Go to Me.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select the account deletion option and confirm the request.

Web Deletion Request

  1. Email support@blessie.ca.
  2. Use the email address associated with your Blessie account when possible.
  3. Include your Blessie username or display name if you know it.
  4. We may ask for information needed to verify that you control the account.

Data Deleted

When your account deletion request is completed, Blessie deletes or anonymizes account-associated data such as:

  • Account profile details, including email, username, display name, and profile photo.
  • Prayer requests, private group posts, group membership, reactions, reports, and block choices.
  • Prayer growth and forest progress connected to the account.
  • Reminder preferences and other account settings.

Data Retained

Some records may be retained for a limited time when needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, abuse prevention, dispute resolution, or backup integrity. Account deletion requests may include a grace period before deletion is completed.

Questions

For account deletion questions, privacy questions, or safety concerns, contact support@blessie.ca.
